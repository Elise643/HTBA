About AT School

AT School is an excellent location to learn. Both our Wyoming and Arkansas campuses are managed by Bill and Arkin Hoopoe. Our standard schedule includes math, science, english, U.S. history, art, dance, self-defense, and most importantly, study hall. Our students live in dorms on campus and have access to an endless selection of food, counselors and a nurse. Our schools are highly selective, and we only offer attendance by invitation. To ensure students get the focus they deserve, we only have one class group per year.
